## Too good to be true
Promises of guaranteed returns, daily profit or "double your money in a week" are the core of investment fraud. Real markets carry risk, and SEBI-registered advisers are not allowed to guarantee returns.

## How trading-group scams work
Victims are added to WhatsApp or Telegram "stock tips" groups filled with fake success screenshots. They are asked to install a trading app from a link rather than an app store. The app shows growing fake profits, but withdrawals require more "taxes" or "fees" until the scammers disappear.

## What to do
Invest only through SEBI-registered brokers and apps from official stores. Verify advisers on the SEBI website. Never transfer money to an individual's account for trading.
