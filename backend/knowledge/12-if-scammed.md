## First hour
Call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 (India) or file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Reports made quickly allow banks to freeze the money in the receiving accounts before it is withdrawn.

## Contact your bank
Block your cards and UPI, and ask the bank to flag the transaction as fraud. Note the transaction reference numbers.

## Secure your devices and accounts
Uninstall any app the scammer asked you to install. Change passwords from a different, trusted device and enable two-step verification.

## Keep evidence
Take screenshots of messages, numbers, UPI IDs, payment references and websites. Don't delete the chat. SENTINEL's Report Scam form also lets you warn others anonymously.

## Beware of recovery scams
People who lost money are often targeted again by fake "recovery agents" or "cyber police" who promise to get the money back for a fee. Real police never charge for this.
