## Fee-based fake job offers
Fake recruiters use the names of real companies and ask for a registration fee, training-kit charge, security deposit or background-verification fee before sending an offer letter. Genuine employers do not charge candidates to get a job.

## How task scams work
The offer is simple online work: liking videos, rating hotels or reviewing products. The first few tasks pay small amounts to build trust. Then you are moved to a Telegram group and asked to "invest" or "recharge" to unlock higher-paying prepaid tasks. Withdrawals are blocked until you pay more, and the money is never returned.

## Warning signs
- Salary far above normal for little work, paid daily
- Recruitment only via WhatsApp or Telegram, with no interview
- Any fee, deposit or purchase required before joining
- Requests for Aadhaar, PAN and bank details before an offer

## What to do
Check job listings on the company's official careers page. Never pay to get a job or to withdraw "earnings". Report task-scam groups and numbers at cybercrime.gov.in.
