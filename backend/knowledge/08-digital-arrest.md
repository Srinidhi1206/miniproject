## How the scam unfolds
A caller claims a parcel in your name contained drugs, or your Aadhaar was used for money laundering. You are "transferred" to a fake CBI, customs or police officer on a video call, sometimes in a room made to look like a police station. You are told you are under digital arrest, must stay on camera, tell no one, and transfer money to a "verification account" to clear your name.

## Why it is always fake
Indian law has no procedure called digital arrest. Police and agencies do not conduct arrests or investigations over WhatsApp or Skype video calls, and no government agency asks citizens to transfer money for verification. The Ministry of Home Affairs has issued public advisories about this exact scam.

## What to do
Disconnect the call. Do not transfer money or share bank details. Tell a family member. Report the number at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930, and use Sanchar Saathi to report the fraudulent call.
