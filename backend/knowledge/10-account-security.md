## Use official apps, not links
The safest way to deal with any "account problem" message is to ignore the link and open your bank or service's official app directly. If there is a real issue, it will be shown there.

## Turn on two-step verification
Enable two-step verification on email, WhatsApp, social media and banking. On WhatsApp, set a two-step PIN so nobody can register your number on another phone even with an SMS code.

## Unique passwords
Reusing one password means one leak exposes every account. Use a password manager or long passphrases, and never share passwords with anyone.

## Keep your SIM safe
Unexpected loss of mobile signal can mean someone is swapping your SIM. Contact your operator immediately. Use Sanchar Saathi to check which mobile connections are registered in your name.
